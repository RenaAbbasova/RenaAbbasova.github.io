NavAgro — AI Analytics for Agricultural Land Use in Navarra

FEATURED

Personal research project combining Sentinel-2 imagery (Google Earth Engine), official crop-production statistics and land-use maps for the 7 agricultural regions of Navarra. Finding: between 2021 and 2023 the Nordoccidental region lost 78% of its dry-farmed herbaceous parcels (1,740 → 386) while forest land grew 58%, yet soft wheat production rose 9% — so not simple abandonment (2023 was a severe drought year). Honest benchmark: with only 28 training rows, NDVI alone does not yet beat the historical mean (R² 0.585 vs −0.189). Data engineering from day one: SHA-256 manifest for every raw file, missing values never fabricated, temporal train/test split.

EV Battery Data Pipeline

SYNTHETIC DATA

End-to-end pipeline for electric-vehicle battery telemetry, built on synthetic data from two simulated manufacturers (different timestamp formats, °F vs °C, inconsistent IDs, duplicates, impossible values). Raw → silver → gold data lake: invalid rows go to quarantine with the reason (nothing is silently dropped) and a quality gate stops the run if more than 15% is rejected. Daily time series per vehicle, a degradation trend, a 6-month forecast tested with a time-split backtest against a naive baseline, rule-based recommendations and a salted-hash anonymized dataset. Orchestrated with Prefect, served through a read-only FastAPI REST API (vehicle health, forecast, fleet summary, clear 422 / 404 / 503 errors) and covered by 100 automated tests. One run: 8,418 raw rows → 8,022 clean and 396 quarantined (4.7%), 24 vehicles. Because the data is synthetic, the forecast result confirms the pipeline works, not how real batteries behave.

Fake News Classifier: what does the model really learn?

COMPLETE

A fake-news classifier on the ISOT dataset (38,644 articles after removing 6,252 repeated and 631 empty ones), built to find out what the model is actually reading. It reaches 98.6% accuracy, but a lookup of the subject column alone scores 100% and five writing-style numbers score 80%: it recognises publisher style and topic, not truth. Stress tests confirm it: accuracy falls to 94% over time and to 44% when every true US-politics article is held out of training. Reusable code with train and predict commands, 22 tests and the analysis saved in a notebook. The conclusion in the README: do not use it to decide what is fake.

Price Elasticity and Pricing

LEARNBAY

Price elasticity of demand on 52 products and 676 product-months (Retail Price Optimization dataset). Comparing each product with itself over time gives an elasticity of about -2.4 (a 1% higher price goes with 2.4% fewer units), while pooling all products wrongly gives -0.13. Product-level estimates turn out too noisy to set prices (typical interval about 12 points, some with the wrong sign), the price does not improve next-month predictions, and no optimal price can be computed without unit costs. A rebuilt version of my first analysis; the README explains what changed.

Flight Ticket Prices: how much do models add?

LEARNBAY

300,153 flight bookings (EaseMyTrip, 2022). The class alone explains 88% of the price, so a high R-squared is easy: the analysis looks at the error in rupees and at the R-squared inside each class. Linear models turn out worse than a lookup table of class + airline + route + stops (error 4,633 against 3,330 rupees), while gradient boosting cuts the error by about 40% for known flights and 27% for flights it has never seen (2,660 rupees). A random split flatters the model: for unseen flights the error is 34% higher. Rebuilt from my first version; the README explains what changed.

NavAgro — Analítica de IA para el uso agrícola del suelo en Navarra

DESTACADO

Proyecto personal de investigación que combina imágenes Sentinel-2 (Google Earth Engine), estadísticas oficiales de producción agrícola y cartografía de uso del suelo de las 7 comarcas agrarias de Navarra. Hallazgo: entre 2021 y 2023 la comarca Nordoccidental perdió el 78% de sus parcelas de cultivo herbáceo de secano (1.740 → 386) mientras el terreno forestal crecía un 58%, pero la producción de trigo blando subió un 9%: no es un simple abandono (2023 fue un año de sequía severa).
